Company Customer Support Policy Document
========================================

RETURN POLICY
-------------
Customers may return any unused product within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
Products must be in their original packaging and in unused condition.
To initiate a return, customers should contact support@example.com with their order number.
Sale items are eligible for store credit only, not cash refunds.
Digital downloads and software licenses are non-refundable once activated.

REFUND POLICY
-------------
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at the time of purchase.
If you paid by credit card, the refund may take an additional 3-5 business days to appear on your statement.
For bank transfers, refunds may take up to 10 business days.
Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

SHIPPING POLICY
---------------
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and costs $4.99.
Express shipping takes 2-3 business days and costs $12.99.
Overnight shipping is available for $24.99 and delivers the next business day if ordered before 2 PM EST.
Free standard shipping is available on all orders over $50.
We ship to all 50 US states and to over 30 international countries.
International shipping typically takes 10-14 business days.
Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email.

WARRANTY POLICY
---------------
All products come with a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, or normal wear and tear.
To make a warranty claim, contact our support team with your order number and photos of the defect.
Warranty replacements are shipped free of charge within the continental United States.
Extended warranty plans are available for purchase for an additional 2 years of coverage.

ORDER ISSUES
------------
If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
Take photos of the damaged item and packaging and email them to support@example.com.
We will send a replacement or issue a full refund at no extra cost.
For missing items from your order, contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date.
Order cancellations can be made within 24 hours of placing the order at no charge.
After 24 hours, orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled.

CONTACT INFORMATION
-------------------
Email: support@example.com
Phone: 1-800-SUPPORT (available Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST)
Live chat is available on our website 24/7.
Average response time for emails is within 24 hours on business days.
